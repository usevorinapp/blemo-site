Blemo Support

Blemo helps you spend less time on distracting apps, with Mimo, a little plant that stays healthy when you do.

Questions, problems or feedback? Contact us using this form. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

Why does Blemo need Screen Time access?

Screen Time lets Blemo block the apps you choose and notice when you’ve spent time on them. Apple keeps the details private: Blemo never sees which apps you picked by name, and your usage never leaves your iPhone.

How does Mimo stay healthy?

Time on your distracting apps makes Mimo thirsty, a little within your daily plan and faster once you go past it. Every 30 minutes of Focus, Block Now or an App Break earns a drop, and each drop brings back 25% of Mimo’s health. Every day starts fresh.

How do I change my daily plan?

Open Profile → Daily plan. A new plan starts the next day.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Blemo, then tap Cancel Subscription. To restore a purchase, open Profile → Restore purchases in Blemo.

How do I delete my account?

Contact us with the email address you signed up with, and we’ll delete your account and its data. If you never created an account, your progress lives only on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app.
