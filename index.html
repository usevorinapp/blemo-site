Blemo Support

Blemo helps you spend less time on distracting apps, with Blemo, a little plant that stays happy when you do.

Questions, problems or feedback? Contact us using this form. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

Why does Blemo need Screen Time access?

Screen Time lets Blemo lock the apps you choose and unlock them when you spend a drop. Apple keeps the details private: Blemo never sees which apps you picked by name, and nothing about your app use leaves your iPhone.

How do drops and unlocks work?

Your distracting apps stay locked. Each unlock uses one of Blemo’s drops: 20 minutes, then 15, 10 and 5. Earn a drop with 30 minutes of Focus or 1 hour of Block Now, then tap Water to give it to Blemo. If you lock your apps early, the time left is saved for your next unlock. Every day starts fresh.

What if I need an app urgently?

When Blemo is out of drops, you get two free 2-minute emergency unlocks a day, from Home or right on the blocked app’s screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Blemo, then tap Cancel Subscription. To restore a purchase, open Profile → Restore purchases in Blemo.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete account in Blemo to delete your account and all its data. You can also contact us with the email address you signed up with. If you never created an account, your progress lives only on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app.
