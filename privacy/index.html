Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Blemo (“we”, “us”) makes the Blemo app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: your Screen Time data stays on your iPhone, an account is optional, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Screen Time data stays on your iPhone

Blemo uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings) to lock the apps you choose and to unlock them when you spend a drop. Apple gives Blemo only private tokens for the apps you pick, not their names, and your unlocks are counted on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Without an account

You can use Blemo without creating an account. Your progress, such as your onboarding answers, focus sessions, Blemo’s health, drops and unlocks, and your streak, is stored only on your iPhone.

If you create an account

You can sign in with Apple, Google or email to back up your progress. We then store:

  • Account details: your email address and the name you give, so you can sign in. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email.
  • Your progress: your onboarding answers (such as your goals, daily plan and the times of day you find hardest), your name in the app, and your focus sessions (when they started and ended, their length and activity), so you can restore them on another device.

This data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, and is used only to run your account.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to know whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives purchase information from Apple and an app user ID (your account ID if you’re signed in). We never see your payment details.

App analytics

We use PostHog to understand how Blemo is used, so we can improve it. The app sends a small number of events: opening the app, screens viewed, finishing onboarding, focus sessions started and finished, unlocks used, drops given to Blemo, Block Now started, and paywall views and purchases. Events include details like a session’s length, never the names of your apps or anything you type. If you’re signed in, events are linked to your account ID. We don’t record your screen. These events are not used for advertising and are not combined with data from other companies.

Crash reports

If Blemo crashes or runs into an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical details such as the error, the app version, your iPhone model and iOS version. It doesn’t include your name, email address or IP address, and it isn’t linked to you.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Blemo lets you know when it needs water, at most twice a day, and gives you a heads-up a minute before your apps lock again. They are created on your iPhone; no notification content is sent to us.

No tracking, no selling

We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, we do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep account data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all data stored with it at any time in the app, in Profile → Delete account. You can also contact us with the email address you signed up with, and we will delete it within 30 days. Data stored only on your iPhone is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Blemo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information. Contact us and we’ll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
